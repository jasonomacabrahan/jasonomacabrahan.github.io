Mabuhay Group ERP (BOMS)

A business operations management system that brings HR, payroll, finance, fleet, documents, and branch operations for Mabuhay Group into one platform, used across every department daily.

Visit mabuhaygroup.com Public careers portal
Industry
Driving education, multi-branch operations
Engagement
Employment, in-house team lead
Delivered
Multi-module web ERP

Challenge

HR, payroll, fund releases, fleet, and document routing ran on spreadsheets, paper forms, and manual logbooks. Information sat in different places, and paper trails were hard to follow.

Solution

One Laravel and Vue.js platform with a module for each department, shared employee and branch records, multi-level approval workflows, and a full audit trail.

My role

As System Developer Team Lead at TL Mabuhay Driving Lesson Academy, Inc., I led system development, coordinated the development team, and oversaw implementation of the ERP, HR, payroll, fleet, and document management modules.

Outcome

  • Used daily across every department
  • Payroll computation and payslips automated, removing spreadsheet errors
  • Every fund release goes through multi-level approval with an audit trail
  • Paper document trails replaced with tracked office-to-office routing

Key features

  • HRIS: employee records, leave, performance data, and approvals
  • Payroll: salary computation, tax deductions, allowances, and payslips
  • Recruitment: job postings, applicant tracking, onboarding, and a public careers page
  • Treasury and disbursing: fund transfer requests, sequential signatory approvals, bank reconciliation, and cash flow reports
  • Document tracking: routing, status, and office-to-office processing history
  • Fleet: vehicle registry, trips, driver assignments, and maintenance records
  • Project management: Kanban task board with full audit history
  • Operations: cooperative finance and quarry management modules

Technology

Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL, REST API

Module screens are mockups with sample data, to keep client information private.

NIMOS: Utility ERP and Mobile Meter Reading

A web ERP and Flutter field app for New Bataan Water Services Cooperative (NEBAWASCO), covering consumer records, meter reading, billing, payments, and reporting.

Visit NIMOS
Industry
Water utility cooperative
Engagement
IT consulting client
Delivered
Web ERP and Flutter mobile app

Challenge

Meter readers recorded consumption on paper, and every reading had to be encoded by hand before bills could go out. Billing took days.

Solution

A Laravel and Vue.js ERP for billing, payments, consumer records, and reporting, paired with a Flutter app that readers use in the field. Readings sync to the server, and billing is generated automatically.

My role

IT consultant to NEBAWASCO. I led development of the NIMOS ERP platform and the Flutter meter-reader app that replaced paper-based field operations.

Outcome

  • Paper meter reading replaced by the mobile app
  • Billing processing cut from days to minutes
  • Consumers, readings, billing, and payments managed in one system

Key features

  • Readers download their zone's accounts and sync completed readings back to the server
  • Reading entry with the previous reading shown for reference
  • Consumer classification: residential and Class A to C
  • Statement of account generation, billing, and payments
  • Dashboard for revenue, active consumers, reader activity, and zone summaries
  • Public website with online bill viewing

Technology

Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL, REST API, Flutter, Dart

Renta: Rent Collection and Property Management

Property and rent management for a Chicago landlord. Tenants pay online, invoices generate with proration, and late fees apply automatically under lease-level rules.

Download APK Android, v1.0.0, 65.3 MB
Industry
Residential property management
Engagement
Client project, Chicago landlord
Delivered
Tenant and landlord apps, operations console

Renta

Property management services, simplified

  • Row-level security

    PostgreSQL row-level security is the authorization boundary for all data access.

  • Late fees that can't double-charge

    A database index makes late-fee charges idempotent, instead of a check that can race.

  • Confirmed payments only

    ACH payments stay “processing” until Stripe confirms them.

  • Audited changes

    Every charge or waiver is recorded with a mandatory reason.

Challenge

The landlord needed tenants to pay online, invoices that handle proration, and late fees applied consistently under each lease's rules, with a reliable record of every charge and waiver.

Solution

Tenant and landlord apps from one Flutter codebase on Supabase and PostgreSQL, Stripe for payments, and a separate operations console on Cloudflare Workers.

My role

I built the tenant and landlord apps, the database and its security rules, the Stripe payment flows, and the operations console.

Outcome

  • Version 1.0.0 released for Android
  • Invoices, proration, and late fees calculated by the system, not by hand
  • Every charge and waiver recorded with a mandatory reason

Key features

  • Online rent payments through Stripe, including ACH
  • Invoices with proration
  • Automatic late fees under lease-level rules
  • Audit trail with a mandatory reason for every charge or waiver
  • Tenant and landlord apps from one codebase
  • Operations console on Cloudflare Workers

Technology

Flutter, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Stripe, Cloudflare Workers
